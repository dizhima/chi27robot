\section{Initial and Target Plans}
\label{app:revision-scripts}

\subsection{Kitchen Island Sorting}

\paragraph{Initial plan.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: open fridge --- lemon\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- lemon\_2 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- apple\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- close fridge
    \item robot1: mug\_1 $\rightarrow$ sink --- mug\_2 $\rightarrow$ sink
\end{itemize}
\paragraph{Baseline example prompt.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Let robot 0 put lemon 1\&2 and apple 1 to the fridge and close the fridge\\
Let robot 1 move the mug 1 and mug 2 to the sink
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Round 1 target.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: open fridge --- lemon\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- lemon\_2 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- close fridge
    \item robot1: \underline{apple\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- apple\_2 $\rightarrow$ fridge} --- mug\_1 $\rightarrow$ sink --- mug\_2 $\rightarrow$ sink
\end{itemize}
Compared with the initial plan, robot1 stores apple\_1 and the newly introduced apple\_2 before handling the mugs, while robot0 remains responsible for the lemons.
Robot1's fridge access requires the door robot0 opens and closes; the system defers robot0's close-fridge until robot1's apples are placed.
\paragraph{Baseline example prompt.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Let robot 0 put lemon 1\&2 in the fridge and close the fridge\\
Let robot 1 put apple 1\&2 in the fridge first, then move mug 1 and mug 2 to the sink
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Round 2 target.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: lemon\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- lemon\_2 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- close fridge --- \underline{canned\_food $\rightarrow$ cabinet --- condiment $\rightarrow$ cabinet --- close cabinet}
    \item robot1: \underline{open fridge} --- apple\_1 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- apple\_2 $\rightarrow$ fridge --- mug\_1 $\rightarrow$ sink --- mug\_2 $\rightarrow$ sink
\end{itemize}
Compared with the first-round target, robot1 opens the refrigerator before storing both apples and handling the mugs.
Robot0 stores the two lemons and closes the refrigerator before completing two newly introduced pantry-storage tasks, moving the canned food and condiment into the cabinet and then closing it.

\subsection{Breakfast Preparation}

\paragraph{Initial plan.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: milk $\rightarrow$ island --- close fridge --- spoon\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- spoon\_2 $\rightarrow$ island
    \item robot1: bowl\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- bowl\_2 $\rightarrow$ island
\end{itemize}
\paragraph{Baseline example prompt.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Let robot 0 move the milk to the island, close the fridge, then move spoon 1\&2 to the island\\
Let robot 1 move bowl 1 and bowl 2 to the island
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Round 1 target.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: milk $\rightarrow$ island --- close fridge --- spoon\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- spoon\_2 $\rightarrow$ island --- \underline{open cabinet --- cereal $\rightarrow$ island --- close cabinet}
    \item robot1: \underline{orange\_1 $\rightarrow$ island} --- bowl\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- bowl\_2 $\rightarrow$ island
\end{itemize}
Compared with the initial plan, robot0 adds a cereal-retrieval task after completing its existing milk and spoon tasks, while robot1 begins by retrieving the newly introduced orange before continuing with the bowls.
\paragraph{Baseline example prompt.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Let robot 0 move the milk to the island, close the fridge, then move spoon 1\&2 to the island, and finally move the cereal from the cabinet to the island and close the cabinet\\
Let robot 1 move orange 1 to the island first, then move bowl 1 and bowl 2 to the island
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Round 2 target.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item robot0: milk $\rightarrow$ island --- spoon\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- spoon\_2 $\rightarrow$ island --- open cabinet --- cereal $\rightarrow$ island --- close cabinet
    \item robot1: bowl\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- bowl\_2 $\rightarrow$ island --- \underline{orange\_1 $\rightarrow$ island --- orange\_2 $\rightarrow$ island --- close fridge}
\end{itemize}
Compared with the first-round target, robot1 moves orange\_1 after its two bowl-retrieval tasks and retrieves the newly introduced orange\_2 immediately afterward.
The refrigerator remains open through robot0's milk retrieval and is closed by robot1 only after both oranges have been retrieved.
